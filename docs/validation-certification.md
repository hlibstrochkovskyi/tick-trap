## The property and the numerical request

Both high-order references use the SACBF initial domain, actuator box, command
quantum, candidate ordering and held dynamics. They certify actual `h(t)` and
`psi(t)` for every obstacle. Neither reference promises a future feasible input.
The relaxed SACBF comparator proves the same two path properties through a
Taylor sufficient condition. It uses the allowed zero-target feasibility
envelope, rather than a complete slack-penalized QP.

The two polynomial queries differ only in when they stop. Minimum refinement
seeks a declared minimum-enclosure width; sign stopping finishes once a
nonnegative lower bound or negative upper witness settles the predicate. Its
minimum enclosure can remain wider. At equal tolerance and subdivision budget,
both traverse the same proof prefix and classify controls identically. Further
exact refinement cannot reverse a proved sign. See the
[derivation and prior art](certificate-goals.md).

## Paired cost at fixed states and commands

Eight fixed states supply 208 candidate commands. The tighter offline reference
certifies 121 high-order-safe paths, 86 unsafe paths and one unresolved tangent.
Each predicate setting is evaluated five times in seeded shuffled order per
command: 2,704 configurations and 13,520 timed evaluations. Timing includes
constructing the condition and exact serializable evidence; it excludes command
search, action selection and the offline reference. Every repeat must return
identical exact evidence.

The main comparison below uses absolute width tolerance `1e-12` and a
64-subdivision limit **per polynomial**:

| Predicate | Accepted | Rejected | Unknown | Reference-safe excluded | Total subdivisions | Median per-control time (µs) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Relaxed SACBF | 48 | 160 | 0 | 73 | 0 | 113.5 |
| Minimum refinement | 121 | 86 | 1 | 0 | 4,069 | 901.9 |
| Sign stopping | 121 | 86 | 1 | 0 | 154 | 265.9 |

SACBF rejection means its sufficient condition failed, whereas polynomial
rejection has a negative path witness. These are not interchangeable failure
labels. The 73 excluded reference-safe commands quantify a gap for this finite
set and this specialized condition, not the volume of the admissible input set
or the strongest possible implementation of the paper.

For each of the 208 matched commands, form the ratio of its median minimum-query
time to median sign-query time. The median of those ratios is `4.48`; individual
ratios range from `0.79` to `11.83`. This single-host timing experiment does not
establish a universal speedup, statistical confidence bound or hard deadline.
The subdivision reduction is deterministic for these exact inputs; elapsed
time also reflects evidence construction and machine behavior. Sign stopping
does not supply the same minimum precision as full refinement.

## Tight cases must remain honest

The first four audit states alone settled all signs without any subdivision.
They are insufficient to evaluate difficult certification. The expanded study
adds an interior braking minimum and a controlled auxiliary tangency with
positive/negative radius perturbations. The exact factorization and its
independent sign argument are recorded in [the case derivation](certificate-goals.md).

For the nominal held command in the three tangency cases:

| Radius | Mathematical auxiliary sign | At tolerance 1e-6, budget 64 | At tolerance 1e-12, budget 8 | At tolerance 1e-12, budget 64 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `0.599999999` | Strictly positive | Unknown | Unknown | Safe, 13 subdivisions |
| `0.6` | Nonnegative, touches zero | Unknown | Unknown | Unknown, 19 subdivisions |
| `0.600000001` | Negative interior value | Unknown | Unknown | Unsafe, 12 subdivisions |

All three nominal paths retain positive geometric clearance. Their auxiliary
property changes. Coarse width tolerance can stop before resolving a small sign
even when more subdivision budget remains. A small work limit can also stop
before a decision. The exact tangent remains computationally unknown at the
reported limits despite a separate analytical factor proof. Neither unknown nor
an approximately zero floating value is silently accepted.

Across all commands at tolerance `1e-12`, zero subdivision budget yields
`112 accepted / 52 rejected / 44 unknown`; budget 8 yields `120 / 85 / 3`;
budget 64 yields `121 / 86 / 1`. Minimum and sign queries have the same counts at
every paired setting. At tolerance `1e-6`, even budget 64 leaves three unknowns.
Thus more work allowance alone cannot compensate for an earlier precision stop.

## Matched control rollouts

The separate eight-second experiment uses four update periods, two initial
positions and two static circles. Every initial state lies in the high-order
domain and has a certified stopping continuation.

| Method | Safe full horizons / 8 | At goal at end / 8 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Relaxed SACBF | 8 | 6 |
| High-order minimum reference | 8 | 8 |
| High-order sign reference | 8 | 8 |

All eight minimum/sign action sequences and exact terminal states match. There
were no observed collisions, unresolved executed intervals or measured deadline
misses. The saved-evidence verifier reconstructed 1,800 holds, 1,200 SACBF
auxiliary checks and 4,800 high-order reference certificates. It checks declared
goals, stopping flags and configured subdivision limits. Tests reject altered
proofs and an action authorized by missing or unresolved evidence.

The polynomial controllers each evaluated 3,772 certificates across accepted
and rejected candidates. Minimum refinement used 779 subdivisions; sign stopping
used one. For the representative period `0.1`, initial position `(-2,0.3)` run,
median controller times were `877.2 µs` and `854.4 µs`, respectively. These are
single rollouts in a fixed run order. They do not inherit the paired predicate
study's time ratio. Most rollout checks were already easy, and whole calls also
include search and record construction. No profiling claim about the dominant
cost is made.

The relaxed filter's safe stationary case was already documented in the
[previous validation](validation-sampling.md). The tighter path condition avoids
it in these tasks. This neither guarantees task convergence in general nor
establishes recursive feasibility for the polynomial reference.

```sh

PYTHONPATH=src python -m tick_trap planar-sweep --config configs/high-order-baselines.json --output results/high-order-baselines

python scripts/summarize-certification.py
```

Run timing experiments separately from replay or other compute-heavy work.
The summary script checks matched configurations and action sequences, exact
goal flags, proof-prefix consistency and the rollout verification input hash.
It requires clean recorded source snapshots. Timing values and artifact hashes
will change on another run.

All safety arguments assume exact state/model, static inflated circles,
componentwise bounded rational commands and exact zero-order-hold execution.
The two references preserve both sets during each executed hold; they do not
make those assumptions valid for a physical robot. Replay uses the existing
polynomial engine and checks artifact consistency, not an independent numerical
implementation or physical-model validity.

This comparison establishes a useful engineering baseline for certificate cost.
It does not establish publication-level novelty. Broader validated-algebra and
continuous-collision-detection comparisons, complete selected paper experiment
reproductions, robust uncertainty and an independently justified learning study
remain research gates.

The current polynomial references explicitly check both `h` and `psi`. With
`h(0)>=0`, nonnegative `psi` already implies nonnegative `h` by the differential
comparison argument. Thus the numerical `h` check is mathematically redundant
for that implication. Its independent evidence is useful, but these timings do
not describe the cheapest possible implementation. Measuring a version that
uses this implication, while retaining the offline geometric oracle, is a
further engineering comparison before claiming an optimized trade-off.

That auxiliary-only engineering comparison is now recorded in the
[audit ledger](validation-audit.md), together with the finite-work decision
differences and its remaining unresolved exact tangency. It establishes no
publication-level novelty.
